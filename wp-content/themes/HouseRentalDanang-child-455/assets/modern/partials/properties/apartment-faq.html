Apartment rental FAQs

Quick answers for comparing apartment options.

How much does it cost to rent an apartment in Da Nang per month?

Typical long-term rents are around VND 7–12 million/month for a studio, VND 10–18 million for a 1-bedroom, and VND 14–25 million for a 2-bedroom apartment. Newer buildings, sea views, swimming pools, larger units, and prime locations near My Khe Beach can cost significantly more.

What is included in the rent, and which costs are extra?

This varies by property. Furnished apartments normally include the furniture and appliances listed in the handover inventory. Some serviced apartments also include Wi-Fi, cleaning, or building services.

Electricity, water, management fees, parking, internet, and cleaning may be charged separately. Before signing, ask for a written breakdown of the total monthly cost so you can compare apartments accurately.

Can foreigners rent an apartment in Da Nang?

Yes. Foreigners can legally rent apartments in Da Nang. You will normally need a valid passport and appropriate entry or residence documents.

The landlord or accommodation manager is also responsible for registering the foreign tenant’s temporary residence with the authorities. Before paying a deposit, confirm that the landlord can complete this registration and has the legal right to rent out the property.

What deposit and lease terms should I expect?

A one-month security deposit is common, although some landlords may request two months, particularly for larger or higher-value apartments.

Long-term leases are commonly 6 or 12 months, while many serviced apartments also accept 3-month stays. Rent may be paid monthly or several months in advance depending on the agreement.

Check the contract carefully for the deposit refund conditions, notice period, early termination rules, utility charges, damage deductions, and payment schedule before signing.

Where do most expats live in Da Nang?

My An and An Thuong are popular with many expat apartment renters because they combine easy beach access with restaurants, cafés, serviced apartments, and a walkable lifestyle.

Phuoc My and My Khe suit people who want to live close to the beach, while An Hai offers a balance between the beach and the city centre.

Hai Chau can be more practical for people who work or spend most of their time downtown. For families or anyone looking for a quieter environment and more space, Khue My, Hoa Hai, and the southern part of Da Nang can offer better value.

For a long-term stay, compare not only location and advertised rent but also noise levels, building quality, total monthly costs, commute time, and nearby everyday services.

Is Da Nang expensive to live in when renting an apartment?

It depends mainly on your rent, the area you choose, and which utilities or services are included. Treat the advertised rent as only one part of your monthly housing cost, then check electricity, water, management fees, parking, internet, cleaning, food, and transport before comparing apartments.

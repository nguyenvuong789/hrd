# 9. Renter fit

* **Strong fit for:** renters who want an apartment location on the east side of the Han River, with direct access to the Tran Hung Dao corridor and on-site facilities including a pool, gym, parking, mini mart and food-and-beverage options. [S1, S3, S4]
* **Possible fit for:** singles, couples and households looking for studio or one- to three-bedroom layouts; exact available formats, size and furnishing need unit-level confirmation. [S6, S7]
* **Main trade-offs:** the development contains multiple blocks/sub-blocks, so layout, floor, view and practical conditions can differ materially between apartments. Current rental pricing and policies cannot safely be generalized from portal listings.
* **Checks before signing:** confirm exact block/tower and unit, usable area, furniture inventory, view/balcony, current rent and inclusions, deposit, lease period, management/parking charges, utilities, pet and guest rules, move-in procedure and authority of the lessor.

# HRD Email Lead Capture Plan (Before Inventory Is Ready)

Updated: 2026-08-30

## Objective

Until HouseRentalDanang has enough reliable listings, do not sell contact credits or promise live availability. Use the current site to build a permission-based email list of foreign renters and learn their move-in timing, area, budget and housing problem.

The temporary offer is:

> Get useful Da Nang rental updates and a shortlist when homes matching your move-in date become available.

This is a lead-generation phase, not a marketplace launch.

## Who to capture

Use two primary signup paths:

1. **Already living in Da Nang** — needs to move within 7-30 days, lease ending, current home problem or wants a better area.
2. **Arriving soon** — planning to arrive in 1-90 days and needs a furnished or long-term rental.

Keep digital nomad, student, family and retiree as tags collected through one optional question. Do not create separate products until the list shows enough demand.

## Lead magnets that fit the current site

Start with one strong useful asset, then add variants only when the first converts:

### 1. Da Nang Rental Starter Pack (primary)

One concise English PDF/web guide:

- Area comparison: An Thuong/My An, Son Tra, Hai Chau and Ngu Hanh Son.
- Typical rent bands in USD and VND.
- Questions to ask before paying a deposit.
- Short-term stay first, then view in person.
- Lease, utilities, temporary residence and internet checklist.
- Move-in planning timeline: 30/14/7 days.

CTA: `Send me the free Da Nang Rental Starter Pack`.

### 2. Move-in timing alert (highest strategic value)

Signup for:

> Email me when rentals become available for my move-in window.

Collect preferred date/month, area, budget, bedrooms and email. Until inventory is ready, send useful market/area updates rather than pretending there are matching homes.

### 3. Living-in-Da-Nang move checklist

For residents: `Moving out soon? Get the 7-day Da Nang rental search checklist.`

This segment has stronger urgency, so place it on `/contact-us/`, `/renting-requests-form/` and relevant apartment archives.

### 4. “Which Da Nang area fits me?” mini quiz

Keep it simple: 5 questions, email results. Outcomes can be An Thuong/My An, Son Tra, Hai Chau or Ngu Hanh Son. Use the result to tag the subscriber; do not claim an objective best neighborhood.

### 5. Price and availability watchlist

An email form for visitors who are not ready to send a rental request:

`Tell us your area, budget and move-in month. We will email useful updates when the market changes.`

## Existing page changes

### Homepage

Keep the current H1 and local-help positioning, but add a secondary email CTA below the first search/property section:

> Not ready to choose a home yet? Get the free Da Nang Rental Starter Pack and move-in updates.

Buttons:

- `Get the free rental guide`
- `Join move-in alerts`

Do not put a fake “available now” promise in this CTA.

### `/renting-requests-form/`

Keep the existing detailed form as the high-intent route. Add:

- `Where are you living now?` — Da Nang / elsewhere in Vietnam / overseas.
- `When do you need to move?` — within 7 days / 8-30 days / 1-3 months / later / just researching.
- `Email me useful rental updates` checkbox, enabled only with clear consent.
- A short expectation note: `We are building a carefully checked rental list. We will only claim availability when we have a recent update.`

Do not make every field required. Email + move-in window + area + budget are enough for a lead.

### `/contact-us/`

The current form already asks for name, email, phone and requirements. Add a visible low-commitment option:

> Just researching? Get the free rental guide and updates by email.

Link to a short signup form; do not force a phone number for an email lead.

### `/why-us/`

Add one section explaining what subscribers receive:

- New area and price guides.
- Rental process warnings.
- Future checked-listing alerts.
- No spam and easy unsubscribe.

Avoid saying “we will find a current home” until the operator can reliably do so.

### `/faqs/`

Add questions:

- `Do I need to be ready to rent?` — No, the guide and updates are free.
- `Are listings guaranteed?` — No; availability changes quickly and must be confirmed.
- `How often will you email?` — Start with at most one useful email per week.
- `Can I unsubscribe?` — Yes, in every email.

### Existing guides

Place inline signup blocks in:

- Coworking/digital-nomad guide: flexible-stay and arrival-date alert.
- Contract guide: deposit/contract checklist download.
- Landlord guide: `before you contact a landlord` checklist.
- Area articles: area-specific move-in alert.

## Email sequence

### Email 0: immediate delivery

Subject: `Your Da Nang rental starter pack`

Deliver the promised guide. Ask one reply question: `Are you already in Da Nang, or arriving later?`

### Email 1: 2 days later

Subject: `The first decision: where in Da Nang should you look?`

Give a concise area comparison and link to the relevant existing pages.

### Email 2: 5 days later

Subject: `Before you pay a rental deposit in Vietnam`

Share the view-first, contract, deposit and utility checklist. This addresses the strongest external research pain without selling fear.

### Email 3: 9 days later

Subject: `What is your move-in window?`

Ask for date/month, budget, area and lease length. Link to the rental request form.

### Email 4: weekly digest only when useful

Possible content:

- Newly published area guide.
- A small set of genuinely current/clearly dated listings once available.
- Rent/area observations with source/date.
- One practical renter tip.

Never pad the digest with stale listings.

## Minimum data model

Store only what is needed for segmentation and follow-up:

- Email.
- Preferred language.
- Current location: Da Nang / Vietnam / overseas.
- Move-in window.
- Preferred areas.
- Budget band.
- Bedrooms/property type.
- Lease length.
- Source page/UTM.
- Consent timestamp and consent wording.
- Status: new / engaged / rental request / converted / unsubscribed.

Do not collect phone/address unless needed for a rental request. Keep the public email promise separate from any future contact-credit product.

## Metrics for the first 30 days

- Signup conversion rate by page.
- Resident vs arriving share.
- Percentage providing a move-in date.
- Email open and click rate.
- Replies to the one-question email.
- Rental request rate from subscribers.
- Unsubscribe/spam complaint rate.
- Cost per qualified email if any ads are tested.

Initial learning thresholds, not permanent benchmarks:

- At least 3% signup rate on high-intent pages.
- At least 40% of new subscribers provide a move-in window.
- At least 25% open rate for the welcome sequence.
- At least 10 meaningful replies or rental requests before creating another lead magnet.

## One-person weekly operating load

- 30 minutes: review new leads and tag them.
- 60 minutes: answer replies and route urgent rental requests.
- 60 minutes: improve one guide or email.
- 30 minutes: send a digest only if there is useful content.
- 30 minutes: review metrics and unsubscribe issues.

AI can draft the guide, translate it, personalize emails by segment, classify replies and prepare a weekly digest. The human must approve claims about price, availability, legal matters and current listings.

## What not to do yet

- Do not launch Contact Pass with thin inventory.
- Do not run a generic newsletter with no renter utility.
- Do not promise weekly available homes if you cannot supply them.
- Do not gate all useful property information behind email; trust comes first.
- Do not collect phone numbers for a low-intent guide download.
- Do not send scraped or copied owner contact data by email.

## Temporary funnel

`Google / existing guide / property page -> useful free guide or move-in alert -> welcome emails -> rental request -> future checked-listing notification -> paid contact test when inventory is ready.`
